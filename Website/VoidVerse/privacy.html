Privacy Policy

This page explains the information VoidVerse may use to operate the service.

Information you provide

This may include your username and email address when you create or update an account. Passwords should be handled by a secure production identity system, never stored in browser code.

Information created while using VoidVerse

The service may process game sessions, chat messages, published experiences, moderation reports, and preference settings to provide features and safety tools.

How information is used

We use information to sign you in, deliver account recovery, operate multiplayer features, moderate the community, and improve the service.

Sharing

Do not make personal information public. VoidVerse should only share information with service providers needed to operate the platform or when required by law.

Children and family safety

VoidVerse is intended to be used with a parent or guardian when required by local law. A production launch should add age-appropriate consent, parental controls, and a verified support process.

Your choices

You can update your username, request password recovery, change privacy settings, and ask the administrator about deleting account information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the VoidVerse administrator through the project’s official support channel.
